# Daily problem report: 2026-11-19

## Problem
**A hijab or long top clings to trousers and leggings when walking, and the usual fixes do not work for many women.**

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Who:** hijab wearers in long tops, jebbas or abayas worn over trousers, leggings or jeans. Every time they walk, especially in wind or dry air.
- **Real voices, Algerian dialect (Maghreb proxy), one TikTok video (68.8K views).** Translations are mine.
  - Repeated: the hold-down fix does not work, 5+ commenters. "I did the *masak* [hold-down garter] and it didn't work." / "The first fix still stuck when I did it." / "I tried all the ways, none worked." / "I tried the *masasik* and it didn't work for me, I don't know why."
  - A woman describes being out with her father and the hijab sticking to her pyjama trousers "so I walked awkwardly" (laughing).
  - Wind: "and when the wind comes..." Another: "slim jeans only stick when there's wind."
  - Winter: "if you wear wide trousers in winter, where does the warmth come from?"
  - Source: [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@3ahd.wear/video/7679786077061664021). Comments pulled earlier; I could not re-open the page today.
- **No Moroccan source found.** Nothing from Moroccan women on this.
- **Cause (general):** synthetic fabrics cling more, and static builds up in dry air and with friction ([Mawdoo3, Arabic](https://mawdoo3.com/كيف_تتخلص_من_كهرباء_الملابس), [Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/washers-and-dryers/how-to-remove-static-from-clothes.html)). I did not verify this is the cause of these women's clinging.

## What women use today, and why it is not solved well
- **Women's own fixes (same video):**
  - Linen or cotton layer under the top.
  - Jeans or leather trousers ("leather ones never stick to my hijab in 4 years").
  - A short jebba (tunic) layer under the top.
  - Moisturizer on the legs, mentioned by one commenter. I make no claim about it.
  - Hold-down garters (the thing many say fails).
- **On sale elsewhere:** anti-static slips and lining (La Redoute "Anti-Static Slip", [Redoute](https://www.laredoute.fr/pplp/100/157878/218/157916/cat-344.aspx)), under-abaya linings ([Chic and Modesty](https://chicandmodesty.com/en/collections/dessous-de-robes)), fabric anti-static spray (for satin dresses per [Lovau](https://lovau.com/blogs/news/how-to-stop-static-cling-in-silk-and-satin-pleated-skirts)). None targets hijab plus trousers.
- **Why not solved (review evidence for slips, US, mixed):**
  - Heat: slip shorts called "so hot", "swamp zone" ([Zappos, Jockey slipshorts](https://www.zappos.com/product/review/8437625/page/13)).
  - Too short for longer skirts or tops: "just didn't look right and was too short."
  - Waistbands fold or roll, and some are still see-through.
  - A heavy slip adds heat, and extra layers feel bad in a hot Moroccan summer (my inference).
- Which women have the problem in Morocco, and in which season, is unknown.

## Solution ideas
**A. A long, breathable slip made to stay smooth under hijab-length tops.**
- **What:** ankle-length, light cotton-modal or viscose-type lining (not nylon), a waistband that does not roll, two or three lengths.
- **Why it fits:** commenters already use cotton or linen under layers and say they work. Reviews of slips complain about heat, roll-up and length.
- **Keywords:** 防静电衬裙 长款, 打底裙 内衬 女 长款, 穆斯林 内衬裙 / "anti static slip long", "under abaya lining", "modest underskirt long". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** heat, length and size returns on COD, and big competitors in lingerie. Claim only "smooth lining, less clinging" after you test it on camera. No "anti-static proof" claim.

**B. Short half-slip (waist to knee) in cotton blend, 2 lengths.**
- **What:** a lighter, cheaper layer that sits between the top and trousers.
- **Why it fits:** a commenter says a short jebba layer under the top stops it sticking, and another says a skirt layer works. It adds less heat than a long slip.
- **Keywords:** 半身衬裙 棉 防静电, 短款衬裙 / "cotton half slip", "short petticoat anti static".
- **Risks:** it may not cover a long top that clings at the calves. Test with real tops and trousers.

**C. Service: a "what to wear under" guide plus a fit check.**
- **What:** a short Darija and French video and a one-page guide: fabric combos (cotton or linen under, avoid polyester over polyester), plus a WhatsApp fit check by height.
- **Why it fits:** women swap fixes in comments and many say theirs failed. A clear tested guide gets attention, and the fit check cuts size returns.
- **Risk:** your time. Only useful alongside a product.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Ask first.** Post the question in a few Moroccan hijab-styling groups or on your page: "Does your hijab or jebba stick to your trousers? What do you do?" Count replies and save the repeated fixes. Check Jumia and Instagram for "jupon", "fond de robe", "sous-abaya" and note prices in MAD. I have not checked them.
2. **Test on camera.** Buy 2-3 popular slips and 2 samples from 1688. Film a 10-second walk with a long top over leggings, with and without each, ideally on a dry or windy day. Keep only what clearly helps.
3. **Pre-order.** Show the winner in the clip and take 10 pre-orders with COD. Ask 20 women what they would pay and what length they need.
