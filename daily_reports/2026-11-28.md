## Solution ideas
**A. A fabric hip-band set with a real grip.**
- **What:** 3 fabric loop bands in different resistances with an inner anti-slip strip, in a small bag.
- **Why it fits:** latex smell and rolling are the repeated complaints, and fabric bands avoid both.
- **Keywords:** 布质 弹力带 臀腿 防滑 三件套, 翘臀圈 防滑 硅胶条 / "fabric resistance bands hip circle anti slip set".
- **Risks:** crowded category. The top Jumia set already says "anti slip". Quality of stitching and the silicone strip varies. Do not make body or health claims (no "slimming", "fat loss").

**B. A small-apartment kit with a workout plan.**
- **What:** bands plus sliders in one bag, with a QR code to a Darija or French plan in 10-minute sessions.
- **Why it fits:** the Moroccan creator shows women want routines with almost no equipment. The plan is the real value.
- **Keywords:** 家用 健身 套装 弹力带 滑盘 / "home workout kit resistance bands sliders".
- **Risks:** plan content takes work, you must avoid medical claims, and sliders need a floor type that works.

**C. Service: women-only home-workout plans by WhatsApp or video.**
- **What:** a paid or free plan in Darija. It could be a bundle perk.
- **Why it fits:** creators with million-view reels show demand, but those are free. A paid service competes with free content, so it only works as a bundle.
- **Risk:** your time; no health claims; needs a qualified coach.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Read Jumia reviews by hand.** Open the 3.9/5 hip-band set and the 11-band kit, filter to 1-2 stars, note repeated words (smell, snapped, rolled, slips). I could not read them.
2. **Test samples.** Buy the top Jumia hip-band set and 2-3 fabric-band samples from 1688. Do 20 minutes of squats and glute work and watch for rolling and slipping. Film a 10-second comparison.
3. **Ask 20 women:** "Do you work out at home? What do you use? Have you bought bands? What went wrong?" Take 10 pre-orders if there is a clear complaint.
