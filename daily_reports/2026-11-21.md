# Daily problem report: 2026-11-21

## Problem
**Women feel unsafe walking alone at night and on transport in Morocco, and the safety devices they could carry are legally unclear, badly rated, or not sold locally in a usable form.**

**Read this first:** safety is the riskiest topic in the queue. No product removes the danger. Do not market anything as "protects you". I am not giving legal advice; check any device with a Moroccan lawyer and customs before you buy stock.

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Moroccan data (strong for the problem itself):** the HCP national survey (2019, 12,000 women and girls) found 13% of women were subjected to violence in public places in the last 12 months (16% urban, 7% rural). Of those cases, about 49% were sexual harassment, 32% psychological, 19% physical. 75% of women think violence in public spaces has increased. [Medias24](https://www.medias24.com/hausse-des-violences-sexuelle-et-economique-a-l-egard-des-femmes-au-maroc-hcp-6107.html), [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/2019/12/11/plus-dune-marocaine-sur-deux-a-ete-violentee-lors-des-douze-derniers-mois_1660549), [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/113636-hcp-57-de-la-population-feminine-a-subi-au-moins-un-acte-de-violence.html) (as summarized by search; I did not open the HCP tables). Public space in this survey includes streets, stations and beaches, not transport alone. The figures are from 2019.
- **Moroccan press:** women's fear of reporting harassment on public transport ([TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/2018/06/15/mary-crass-les-femmes-nosent-pas-rendre-compte-dune-agression-dans-le-transports-de-peur-des-represailles_1598859)); half of Casablanca tram riders are women ([Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/232983-232983.html)).

## What women use today, and why it is not solved well
- **Today:** phone call to someone, keys in hand, avoiding transport at night. I found no Moroccan women's own list of what they carry.
- **Pepper spray is illegal in Morocco, per one source.** An aujourdhui.ma article says tear-gas and pepper sprays are treated as "suffocating devices", banned under penal code articles 303 and 303 bis, and sold informally anyway ([aujourdhui.ma](https://aujourdhui.ma/?p=263299), as summarized by search; I did not read the law). A 2020 law (n° 10-20) also regulates defense and security equipment ([adala.justice.gov.ma](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2025/10/17/Dahir%20n%C2%B0%201-20-70%20du%204%20hija%201441%20portant%20promulgation%20de%20la%20loi%20n%C2%B0%2010-20%20relative%20aux%20mat%C3%A9riels%20et%20%C3%A9quipements%20de%20d%C3%A9fense%20et%20de%20s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9%2C%20aux%20arm-1760708296306.pdf)). I did not read what it says about alarms. **Do not sell sprays.**
- **Personal alarms:** I found no real personal-attack alarm sold on Jumia or Marjane Mall. What exists: a keychain with whistle and alarm at 59 DH on Marjane Mall ([Marjane Mall](https://www.marjanemall.ma/p/porte-cles-avec-sifflet-et-alarme-outil-pour-trouver-vos-cles-perdues-perte-aaamq34559)), and key-finder LED whistles around 29 DH on Jumia ([Jumia](https://www.jumia.ma/mlp-k-anti-perte-cle/)). ## Solution ideas
**A. A rechargeable personal alarm built around the review complaints.**
- **What:** a keychain alarm with a loud siren, a safety cover or two-step trigger so it does not go off by accident, a USB charge, and a small light.
- **Why it fits:** abroad, the top complaints are accidental triggering, dead batteries and inflated loudness claims.
- **Keywords:** 防狼报警器 女 充电, 个人安全警报器 钥匙扣, 防身警报器 / "personal safety alarm keychain rechargeable". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks (legal first):** check whether Law 10-20 or customs rules cover it. Test the real loudness. Rechargeable batteries have shipping limits. Never use "protection", "self-defense weapon" or fear-based ads.

**B. Phone tether: crossbody and wrist strap for a phone.**
- **What:** an adjustable phone strap so the phone is in reach for a call and harder to snatch.
- **Why it fits:** women say they call someone while walking, and Moroccan news reports bag and phone snatching ([TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2026/08/13/marrakech-un-vol-a-larrachee-finit-en-accident-mortel_2003308/)). I found no woman asking for this.
- **Keywords:** 手机挂绳 斜挎 可调节, 手机防丢绳 手腕 / "phone lanyard crossbody adjustable", "phone wrist strap".
- **Risks:** weak straps and clips, phone-case fit. **Do not claim "anti-theft".** Overlaps with the pouch idea from 2026-11-18.

**C. Channel idea: sell through universities and women's associations.**
- **What:** packs sold or given via campus groups and NGOs.
- **Why it might fit:** Oxfam Morocco ran a tram campaign on violence against women, and CARE works on girls' safety. I do not know if they want products. This is a guess.
- **Risks:** reputation, and NGOs may refuse to promote devices. Needs the legal check first.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Legal and competitor check first.** Ask a Moroccan lawyer or customs broker about personal alarms under Law 10-20. Search Jumia, Avito, Instagram and Facebook Ad Library for "alarme d'agression", "alarme de défense", "صافرة إنذار". Check what is already sold and at what price.
2. **Test samples for real.** Buy 3 popular alarms and 2 samples from 1688. Measure volume with a phone app, test accidental triggering in a bag, run the battery for a week. Film a 10-second demo.
3. **Ask 20 women (18-30):** "What do you carry when you go home at night? Would you carry a small alarm? What would put you off?" Then take 10 pre-orders only if the legal answer is clear.
