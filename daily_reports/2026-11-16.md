# Daily problem report: 2026-11-16

## Problem
**Heels, boots and flats slip at the back and rub the heel, and the heel grips sold to fix it fall off or leave sticky residue.**

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Who:** women who wear heels, boots, ballerina flats or new shoes. Comes back every time they wear them, so it repeats weekly for many women.
- **Moroccan workaround (real):** a Moroccan creator folds a napkin inside her heeled boots. 2.6M IG views. Caption "girls, try it" (my translation). [IG](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfFXKXhN_GW). **No complaint quotes from Moroccan women found.** Instagram comments cannot be read, and Jumia blocks automated access.
- **Cause (proxy, podiatry and footwear sites):**
  - A heel that slips inside the shoe is the main cause of heel blisters. [Michigan Foot Doctors](https://www.michiganfootdoctors.com/shoe-bite-blister/)
  - A stiff back of the shoe that is not yet molded to the heel hurts on new shoes. A chiropractor suggests an anti-slip strip at the back. [info.fr guide](https://info.fr/chaussures-qui-font-mal-a-larriere-guide-complet/) (as summarized by search)
  - Ballerina flats with a gap at the back rub and cause blisters. [Pourquoi Docteur](https://www.pourquoidocteur.fr/Articles/Question-d-actu/44726-Les-ballerines-sont-elles-bonnes-pieds)

## What women use today, and why it is not solved well
- **Today:** folded napkin or tissue (Moroccan hack above), adhesive heel grips, plasters, wearing thicker socks, breaking in shoes (hair-dryer warming, per [consoGlobe](https://www.consoglobe.com/astuces-chaussures-confortables-4392-cg?amp) via search).
- **Why it fails:** the tissue slides and is not designed for the job. Grips fail on adhesion, residue and thickness, and they slip more in boots and with sweaty feet. **Sweat and heat as the cause in Morocco is my guess, not found in a source.** Right sizing is the real fix, and women cannot always try shoes on (online or COD shopping).

## Solution ideas
**A. A better heel liner: thin, soft fabric top, low-residue adhesive, made for sweaty feet.**
- **What:** multi-pair packs in 2 thicknesses (thin for flats, thicker for boots).
- **Why it fits:** targets the repeated complaints: falls off, sticky residue, too thick, too rubbery.
- **Keywords to try on 1688/Taobao:** 防滑后跟贴, 防磨脚后跟贴, 高跟鞋防掉跟贴, 隐形后跟贴 / "heel grips liner", "anti-slip heel sticker", "heel cushion insert". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** adhesive quality varies a lot by factory. You can only learn it by testing in heat and in boots. Small and light, so low shipping and low return cost.
- **Claim risk:** say "stops the heel slipping" only if your test shows it. Do not say "prevents blisters" or anything medical.

**B. "Heel and shoe comfort kit" by shoe type.**
- **What:** a pack matched to flats, boots or heels: grips + a front-of-foot cushion. No blister plasters (health product).
- **Why it fits:** one pack answers "what do I need for this shoe".
- **Keywords:** 前掌垫 防滑鞋垫 硅胶后跟套 / "forefoot cushion", "non-slip insole", "silicone heel cup".
- **Risks:** more items means more sizing mistakes and a higher cost.

**C. Service: shoe-fit helper (content plus WhatsApp).**
- **What:** a short Darija video and WhatsApp advice: "send a photo of your shoe, we tell you which pad". Tells women to check size and break-in too.
- **Why it fits:** the real cause is often fit. It builds trust and cuts COD returns.
- **Risk:** your time. It does not replace a product.
