## Solution ideas
**A. A fit-first bonnet: adjustable, double layer, two sizes.**
- **What:** wide soft band with an adjustable tie or hidden drawstring, double-layer satin-finish fabric, a regular and a large (XL) size, with a size guide.
- **Why it fits:** Jumia reviewers say too big or too small. Abroad, buyers say it slips off, pinches or stretches. A size and an adjustable band answer both.
- **Keywords:** 双层缎面睡帽 可调节 卷发, 宽边睡眠发帽 大号, 缎面发帽 抽绳 / "double layer satin sleep cap adjustable", "satin bonnet XL adjustable". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** crowded, price-driven market. State the real material ("satin-finish polyester" unless it is silk). Sizing mistakes drive COD returns. No hair-health claims. Claim only "stays on overnight" after a timed test.

**B. Night curl kit: bonnet plus large satin scrunchie plus a zip satin pillowcase.**
- **What:** a bundle for different sleep styles.
- **Why it fits:** brand blogs push the pineapple bun and satin pillowcases as alternatives. A kit raises the order value. (My source for this is cosmetic-brand blogs, which sell these things.)
- **Keywords:** 缎面枕套 拉链, 大号缎面发圈 / "satin pillowcase zipper", "satin scrunchie large".
- **Risks:** pillowcase sizes vs Moroccan pillows (unchecked), more items to inspect.

**C. Service: Darija "how to wrap" video plus a fit helper.**
- **What:** a short video (bonnet plus scarf, pineapple bun) and a WhatsApp helper: "send your hair length and your head size, we tell you the size."
- **Why it fits:** the 1.4M-view Moroccan wrap tutorial and women discovering they have curly hair show a need for guidance. The helper cuts COD size returns.
- **Risk:** your time. It does not fix a bonnet that fails.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Read the actual Jumia reviews by hand.** Open 5 bonnet listings. Filter to 1-2 stars. Write down the repeated words: too big, too small, slips, rough. I could not read them.
2. **Test samples.** Buy the 3 best-rated bonnets on Jumia and 2-3 samples from 1688. Wear each for a night, film a time-lapse and a morning check. Keep only a bonnet that stays on.
3. **Ask and pre-order.** Ask 20 women with curly hair what they do at night and what failed. Show the winner and take 10 pre-orders with COD.
