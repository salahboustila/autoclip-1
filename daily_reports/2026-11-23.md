# Daily problem report: 2026-11-23

## Problem
**Trousers and leggings bought online often do not fit at the waist (too big, gap at the back, waistband rolls down), and women fix it with improvised tricks. Cheap fixes already exist on Jumia.**

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Who:** women buying trousers and leggings, especially online, where they cannot try them on. Whenever they wear them.
- **Moroccan voice (one creator, Darija, my translation):** a Moroccan creator rants "nothing fits me anymore" (Darija: "Khouti tahaja mab9at katjini") and shows tightening the waist with a hair clip. 778K IG views. [IG](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcYeeOrMZjy) This is one video, and I could not read its comments.
- **Repeated complaint, leggings (proxy, US, 4 reviewers on Sam's Club listings):** "the waist rolls down constantly", "no elastic around the waist so they slide down and roll down", "the waistband would not stay up", "the waist is too wide so they roll down before you move." [Sam's Club](https://www.samsclub.com/reviews/product/13594724833?page=50) (quotes read in search snippets, not on the page)
- **Sellers know the problem (Moroccan market):** Jumia sells compression leggings advertised as "seamless, anti-roll" ([Jumia compression leggings](https://www.jumia.ma/slp/leggings-de-compression)). That is a seller's claim, not a buyer's complaint. One Jumia legging listing shows 4/5 from 54 reviews ([Jumia leggings](https://www.jumia.ma/leggings-femme/)). I did not find Jumia reviews that mention the waist.
- **Sizing is uneven (weak):** on a Shein Morocco page, one buyer says the size runs a little big and another advises ordering two sizes smaller. That is a men's wide-leg trouser listing, so it only hints at sizing mismatch in general. [Shein Morocco](https://m.shein.com/ma/Men%27s-Plus-Size-Stretch-Suit-Pants%2C-Wide-Leg-And-Extra-Large%2C-Stretch-Fabric%2C-Suitable-For-Daily-Casual-Wear-p-490439113.html)
- **Why it matters when selling online here:** a 2026 Moroccan e-commerce guide estimates 70-80% of payments are cash on delivery ([Jobsquare](https://www.jobsquare.ma/blog/ecommerce-marketplaces-maroc-guide-complet/), vendor blog). Jumia lists free returns within 7 days under conditions and home delivery at about 18 MAD (seen in a search summary of a Jumia product page; check the policy).

## What women use today, and why it is not solved well
- **Today:** a hair clip (the Moroccan creator), a belt, an elastic or drawstring waist, adding elastic or taking in the seams (sewing blogs, for jeans), going to a tailor.
- **Fixes already on Jumia Morocco (seen through search):**
  - Extension buttons, 2 for about 39 MAD ([Jumia waist extender](https://www.jumia.ma/slp/extension-taille-multifonction)).
  - Reusable no-sew adjustable jean buttons, 10-pack ([Jumia adjustable button](https://www.jumia.ma/slp/bouton-de-pantalon-ajustable), [for jeans](https://www.jumia.ma/slp/bouton-ajustable-pour-jeans)).
  - Magnetic pants buttons ([Jumia](https://www.jumia.ma/slp/bouton-magnetique-pour-pantalon)).
  - Elastic belts without buckles ([Jumia belts](https://www.jumia.ma/ceintures-femmes/)).
- **So why do women still use hair clips?** I do not know. It may be that they do not know these products exist, that these products do not work well, or that the creator was just being funny. I found no complaints about the adjusters.
- **Why not solved (sewing sources, foreign):** the real cause is often cut, not size: a wider hip needs a larger seat, leaving a gap at a narrower waist ([Primer](https://www.primermagazine.com/2025/learn/3-fixes-if-you-hate-the-way-your-pants-fit-that-have-nothing-to-do-with-your-waist-size)). Elastic-insert fixes do not work if the pants are several sizes too big ([It's Always Autumn](https://www.itsalwaysautumn.com/how-to-fix-your-jeans-waistband-no-more-jeans-gap.html/comment-page-1)). I found nothing on fixes for knit leggings.

## Solution ideas
**A. A better-presented waist-fix kit.**
- **What:** a small pack mixing a no-sew button adjuster for woven trousers and a clip or adjuster for stretchy waistbands, with a Darija demo and a size guide.
- **Why it fits:** the Moroccan creator uses a hair clip. A before/after clip is very easy to make.
- **Keywords:** 免缝纽扣 可调节 裤腰, 缩腰扣 裤子 调节 神器 / "adjustable jean button no sew", "waist tightener clip pants". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** crowded: Jumia already has them, so price is the battleground (39 MAD for 2 is the anchor I saw). Quality is hard to see. Metal pins can snag fabric. No evidence yet that women are unhappy with existing ones.

**B. Leggings with a waistband that does not roll.**
- **What:** wide high waistband, no-roll finish, size guide by waist and hip measurements.
- **Why it fits:** the 4-reviewer complaint and Jumia sellers already advertising "anti-roll".
- **Keywords:** 高腰 瑜伽裤 防卷边 无缝 / "high waist leggings no roll waistband".
- **Risks:** size returns, crowded category, quality varies by factory. Needs a wear test: do 20 minutes of walking and squats.

**C. Service: measure-first sizing helper for COD.**
- **What:** WhatsApp sizing before shipping: send waist, hip and height, get a size. Optionally a small alteration voucher with a local tailor (hypothesis: tailors are widely used, but I found no source).
- **Why it fits:** wrong size is the root issue and COD returns cost you.
- **Risk:** your time. It does not make the product better.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Read the actual Jumia reviews.** Open the adjuster and anti-roll leggings listings, filter to 1-2 stars, and note repeated words (breaks, slips, damages fabric, too big). If nobody complains, drop idea A. I could not read them.
2. **Test.** Buy 3 popular adjusters from Jumia and 2-3 samples from 1688. Test on woven trousers and on leggings over a day. Film a 10-second clip: hair clip vs adjuster.
3. **Ask 20 women:** "Have you had trousers or leggings too big at the waist? What did you do? Did you try waist buttons?" Take pre-orders only if repeated complaints exist.
