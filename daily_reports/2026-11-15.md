# Daily problem report: 2026-11-15

## Problem
**Hijab pins hurt, get lost, damage the scarf, and the fixes on sale are too expensive.**

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Who:** women who wear hijab, 18-30. Daily use, so it repeats every day.
- **Price complaints (5+ voices, Arab world, one Moroccan).** On a hijab-grip video with 2M+ followers behind it:
  - Top comment, 1.7K likes: "Hope the brand isn't too expensive, can girls of simple means buy?"
  - Moroccan commenter: "Prices very high for Morocco, this piece is 41.5 USD = 415 MAD plus delivery, even scarves are cheaper."
  - "A plastic piece at that price" (10 likes). "I feel it cuts the scarf" (131 likes). "I have hair loss, afraid it gets stuck" (56 likes).
  - Source: [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@shahdzn/video/7678352668871380244). Translations are mine.
- **Pin pain and loss:**
  - A magnet demo with the hook "DO PINS KEEP HURTING YOUR NECK?" got 2.5M views. 6+ commenters ask "price?", "where to buy?", "how to order?". One doubter: "looks like it will choke me, I'll stick with my safety pins." [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@abbaya.byalnisa/video/7683169378719878421)
  - A skit about not finding "that one stupid pin": 1.3M views. [IG](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcYbC70NEEH)
  - A Moroccan creator's story about swallowing a pin at an airport: 5.6M IG views. [IG](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DefRwVER0w6) **Do not reuse her story in ads.**
- **Related, separate problem:** hijab clinging to trousers when walking. 5+ Algerian-dialect commenters say hold-down garters failed. [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@3ahd.wear/video/7679786077061664021)

## What women use today, and why it is not solved well
- **Today:** safety pins, decorative pins (Jumia sells 12 pearl pins for 39 MAD, per search: [Jumia pins](https://www.jumia.ma/mlp-k-epingles/)), magnets, grips, under-caps.
- **Why it fails:**
  - The grip people know costs about 415 MAD (one commenter's figure).
  - Women fear fabric damage and hair tangling.
  - Magnets have doubters. One 1-star Walmart review says they "did not hold the scarf tightly" ([Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/reviews/product/1796954670), seen in search). An SEO blog claims magnets lose grip on thin fabric and thick layers ([AliExpress wiki](https://www.aliexpress.com/s/wiki-ssr/article/hijab-pins-brooches), weak source).
  - Slip-back is often a layering problem. Silky under-caps slip ([Hijabinist](https://hijabinist.com/blogs/our-blogs/why-hijabs-slip-simple-fixes-that-actually-work), brand blog). Tying an under-scarf too tight can make it slip more ([Haute Hijab](https://blog.hautehijab.com/post/customer-generated-questions-about-our-ultimate-underscarves), brand blog).
  - Silicone and stretch bands get "too tight / headache / itchy" complaints in retailer reviews (not hijab-specific, weak evidence).

## Solution ideas
**A. Cheap, strong magnet pin set, sold in MAD.**
- **What:** a pack of magnet pins with a clear promise: no holes, holds 2 layers, safe on thin fabric.
- **Why it fits:** the main complaints are price, pin pain, and fear of cutting the scarf.
- **Search keywords for 1688/Taobao:** 磁吸头巾扣, 磁力头巾夹, 磁吸围巾扣 / "hijab magnet pins", "magnetic scarf clip". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:**
  - Magnet strength varies a lot between suppliers.
  - Strong magnets can face air-freight rules, so check shipping rules.
  - Thin or sheer fabrics may need a stronger or different design.
  - Cheap packs already exist: SHEIN sells many hijab-magnet multipacks, and one foreign shop lists a model at 2.98 USD ([SHEIN example](https://m.shein.tw/12%20Pcs%20Set%20Women%20s%20Hijab%20Magnets%20No%20Snag%20Hijab%20Pins%20Multi%20Purpose%20Magnetic%20Pins%20For%20Hijabs%20Scarves%20Shirts%20Dresses%20And%20Clothes-p-443842450.html)). Whether those ship to Morocco is unverified.

**B. "Stay-put kit" bundle.**
- **What:** magnets, a cotton or jersey under-cap (not silky), and a small pouch.
- **Why it fits:** slip-back often comes from layers, not just pins. A bundle can look better value than a single pin.
- **Keywords:** 防滑头巾底帽, 棉质头巾内搭帽 / "inner hijab cap cotton".
- **Risks:** sizing returns on caps, and COD return costs. Keep the cap one-size and stretchy.

**C. Service: Darija "how to place" video guide with every pack.**
- **What:** a QR code to a 20-second Darija demo, plus a WhatsApp number for questions.
- **Why it fits:** women doubt magnets ("will it choke me?", "will it hold?"). Tutorials get millions of views in this niche.
- **Risk:** low cost, mainly your time. It cannot fix a weak magnet.

**Claim and legal risk:** keep claims to "no holes", "holds X layers" after you test it, and "no pin to lose". No health claims. Do not mention swallowing or neck injuries.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Check Moroccan competitors.** Search Facebook Ad Library, Instagram and TikTok (Arabic "مغناطيس حجاب", French "aimant hijab"), Jumia, Avito. Write down prices in MAD. My search found no Moroccan seller of hijab magnets with a MAD price, but search is not proof.
2. **Test samples on camera.** Order 2-3 samples from different suppliers. Film a 10-second test: chiffon, jersey, 2 layers, head turn and walk. Keep the winner, drop the rest.
3. **Pre-order test.** Post the test video and offer 10 pre-orders by WhatsApp with COD at a price clearly below the 415 MAD anchor. Ask 20 hijab wearers what they would pay and what they fear (cutting, hair, strength).
