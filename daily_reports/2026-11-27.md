# Daily problem report: 2026-11-27

## Problem
**Narrow shoulder-bag and tote straps dig into the shoulder when the bag is heavy.**

**Honest note: this is the weakest topic so far.** I found no Moroccan woman describing it. Treat it as an idea to test, not a finding.

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Who:** women who carry a heavy bag daily: students, workers, commuters. Daily for them.
- **Moroccan evidence:**
  - Jumia sells many shoulder and crossbody bags (255 crossbody products). Some listings advertise a "padded adjustable strap" or a "wide strap for a comfortable shoulder", which shows sellers think comfort sells ([Jumia crossbody bags](https://www.jumia.ma/sacs-bandouliere/), [adjustable crossbody](https://www.jumia.ma/slp/sac-a-bandouliere-ajustable-pour-femme)).

  - **No Moroccan review or quote about straps digging in.** My Arabic searches returned only general health articles (heavy bag on one shoulder, switch shoulders). I make no health claims.
- **Abroad (proxy, brand and media sources, weak):** narrow straps concentrate pressure and wider straps spread it ([NDTV Shopping](https://www.ndtvshopping.com/fashion/why-tote-bags-hurt-your-shoulders-the-strap-width-mistake-most-people-make-10912704), also in the 2026-11-15 research). ## What women use today, and why it is not solved well
- **Today:** switching shoulders, a backpack, a crossbody bag, padded straps built into the bag. Pads sold separately exist abroad.
- **Why it might not be solved:** pads slip, wear out and do not fit every strap. This comes from camera-bag reviews, not from Moroccan women.
- **Not checked:** whether strap pads or wide replacement straps are sold on Jumia. I did not search for them.

## Solution ideas
**A. A wide, non-slip strap pad that fits any strap.**
- **What:** a soft pad with a grippy underside and a wrap-around (hook-and-loop) design, so it goes on without removing the strap.
- **Why it fits:** the fix complaints are slipping, wearing out and needing the strap removed.
- **Keywords:** 包带 肩带 减压垫 防滑 可缠绕, 背包带 护肩垫 / "bag strap pad non slip wrap", "shoulder strap cushion hook and loop". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** small ticket, hard to see the difference in a photo, and no evidence that Moroccan women want it. Claim only "wider and softer", no health claims.

**B. A wide replacement or crossbody strap for totes.**
- **What:** a 4-5 cm wide adjustable strap with clips that turns a shoulder bag into a crossbody bag.
- **Why it fits:** wide straps spread the load, and a fixed 120 cm strap is on a Jumia listing as a limit. Overlaps with the phone-pouch idea from 2026-11-18.
- **Keywords:** 包包 肩带 替换 加宽 斜挎, 可拆卸 加长 背带 / "wide replacement bag strap adjustable", "detachable crossbody strap".
- **Risks:** clip quality and strength, compatibility with bag rings.

**C. A cross-sell, not a separate product.**
- **What:** offer the strap or pad as an add-on when you sell any bag or pouch.
- **Why it fits:** low value alone, but adds order value to products you already sell.
- **Risk:** none until you sell bags.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Check the market.** Search Jumia for "coussinet bandoulière", "protège épaule sac", "bandoulière large amovible", and Instagram. Read the 1-2 star reviews on shoulder bags and note any mention of the strap. If nothing, stop here.
2. **Ask first.** Ask 20 women who carry a bag daily: "Does your bag strap bother you? What do you do?" If few say yes, drop it.
3. **Cheap test.** If yes, buy samples of A and B from 1688, film a 10-second before/after, and take 10 pre-orders.
