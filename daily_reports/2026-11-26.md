# Daily problem report: 2026-11-26

## Problem
**Sleep masks sold in Morocco let light in (especially at the nose) and some buyers say they do not work, so a mask that really blocks light is missing from cheap listings.**

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Who:** women who need darkness to sleep: light in the room, summer mornings, shared rooms, travel. Nightly for those who use a mask.
- **Context only, no claim:** a national study reports about 30% of Moroccans with insomnia symptoms, women twice as likely as men ([Yabiladi](https://en.yabiladi.com/articles/tagged/121783/insomnia.html), as summarized by search; I did not open the study). I make no claim that a mask helps with this. I found no data on light or heat as causes in Morocco.

## Solution ideas
**A. A truly light-tight contoured mask.**
- **What:** deep eye cups (no lash contact), a nose flap or wide nose blocker, soft adjustable strap, two sizes.
- **Why it fits:** the Moroccan complaints are light at the nose and "no darkness".
- **Keywords:** 3D立体 眼罩 睡眠 遮光 鼻梁 防漏光, 眼罩 深眼窝 不压睫毛 / "3D contoured sleep mask nose flap blackout".
- **Risks:** a crowded 44 MAD category. Quality differs by factory. Test it in a dark room with a phone flashlight. Claim only "blocks light" after a real test. No sleep-benefit or health claims.

**B. Blackout kit for the room (hypothesis).**
- **What:** a reusable clip-on or suction blackout panel for windows, sold with the mask.
- **Why it might fit:** experts say a mask helps when a room cannot be darkened. I found no Moroccan source on windows or summer mornings.
- **Keywords:** 遮光 窗帘 吸盘 临时, 免打孔 遮光布 / "temporary blackout window cover suction".
- **Risks:** size and shipping, fit on Moroccan windows, no evidence yet.

**C. Service: a trust promise with a visible light test.**
- **What:** a 10-second dark-room test clip for each mask, a WhatsApp number, and a "light still comes in? we replace it" promise.
- **Why it fits:** Jumia reviews include "scam" and sellers with very poor scores. Trust is a gap.
- **Risks:** replacement cost. You must have a mask that actually passes the test first.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Read the Jumia reviews by hand.** Open 5 mask listings, filter to 1-2 stars, and note repeated words (light, nose, scam, not my face). I could not read them. Check Instagram sellers and Facebook Ad Library.
2. **Test.** Buy 3 popular Jumia masks and 3 samples from 1688 (with nose flaps). Test each in a dark room with a phone flashlight. Wear them for a night. Film the best-vs-worst light test.
3. **Ask 20 women:** "Do you use a sleep mask? What do you dislike (light, pressure, slipping)? Would you pay more for one that works?" Take 10 pre-orders for the winner.
