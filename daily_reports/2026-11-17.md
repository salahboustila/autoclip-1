# Daily problem report: 2026-11-17

## Problem
**Moroccan girls and women feel shame about periods, so they hide pads and tampons, and nothing they carry them in is made to be discreet.**

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Who:** girls and women with periods, school age to 30. Monthly, but the hiding happens every time they carry items or buy them.
- **Moroccan sources (press and campaigns, not women's own posts):**
  - A CARE Morocco report (TelQuel, June 2026) quotes the NGO's director: "As long as girls are ashamed or afraid of having their period at school, we miss an essential lever of development" (French, my translation). [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/2026/06/19/sante-menstruelle-au-maroc-care-veut-faire-de-la-dignite-un-levier-degalite_1995248)
  - A Medias24 report says families do not really prepare girls for their first period, and shopkeepers hide pads in newspaper. [Medias24](https://www.medias24.com/MAROC/SOCIETE/186509-First-blood-Une-serie-sur-les-menstruations-qui-leve-les-tabous.html) (as summarized by search)
  - The #7achak campaign (since 2021): "Here in Morocco it is shameful to go into a grocery and ask the owner for a sanitary pad" (French, my translation). [H24info](https://h24info.ma/?p=351319), [TelQuel 2019](https://telquel.ma/2019/06/15/cavasaigner-la-precarite-menstruelle-est-aussi-une-problematique-marocaine_1641995)
  - A Moroccan woman, first period at 12: her mother gave her an old cloth and sent her to bed without dinner. [UNFPA](https://www.unfpa.org/fr/news/%C3%A9tats-arabes-femmes-et-filles-r%C3%A9v%C3%A8lent-que-les-premi%C3%A8res-r%C3%A8gles-s%E2%80%99accompagnent-souvent-de-peur)

## What women use today, and why it is not solved well
- **Today:** hide pads in a sleeve, pocket or bag, wrap in paper, borrow from a friend, carry in a generic pencil case or makeup bag. No Moroccan quote found describing what exactly women use. That part is my inference.
- **Why not solved:**
  - I found no Moroccan seller of a period pouch. The only price anchors are French waterproof pouches for used pads at 8.90 EUR and 11.90 EUR ([Greenweez](https://www.greenweez.com/produit/petite-pochette-impermeable-turquoise/1TOUD0022)).
  - Moroccan hygiene shops sell the pads and tampons themselves ([Wlidaty](https://wlidaty.com/en/collections/hygiene-intime), [Marjane Mall](https://www.marjanemall.ma/beaute-sante/hygiene/hygiene-intime)). I saw no pouch there.
  - Reviews of period pouches are thin. Generic complaints about small pouches and broken zippers exist ([Staples review](https://www.staples.com/ptd/review/472597)) but not for period pouches specifically.
  - The shame also affects buying: shopkeepers hide pads, and asking is embarrassing. A private online order may solve part of it.

## Solution ideas
**A. A discreet pouch that does not look like what it holds.**
- **What:** opaque, lined, zip pouch, sized for 2-3 pads plus tampons. Looks like a pencil case, key wallet or makeup pouch. Sold as a 3-pack of colors for school, work and gym.
- **Why it fits:** the evidence is about hiding, not about the item. Surveys and Moroccan press say the shame is real.
- **Keywords:** 姨妈包, 卫生巾收纳包, 迷你化妆包 拉链 / "sanitary pad pouch", "period pouch", "mini cosmetic pouch zipper". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** zipper quality, lining that smells, size. Small, light, low return cost. **Do not put the word "period" or product images of pads on the pouch.**

**B. Two-pouch set: a clean pouch plus a waterproof pouch for used items.**
- **What:** an opaque outer pouch plus a small waterproof one inside.
- **Why it fits:** school-advice pages tell girls to carry a water-resistant bag for used items (seen in an Arabic-language search summary; I could not tell which page). French shops already sell waterproof pouches for used pads at 8.90 EUR ([Greenweez](https://www.greenweez.com/produit/petite-pochette-impermeable-turquoise/1TOUD0022)), so there is a price anchor.
- **Keywords:** 防水收纳袋 小号, 湿物袋 / "waterproof wet bag small", "reusable waterproof pouch".
- **Risks:** waterproof coating quality, smell, and washing instructions.

**C. Service: discreet ordering and gifting.**
- **What:** plain, unlabeled parcels and a mother-to-daughter "first pouch" gift set, ordered by WhatsApp with COD.
- **Why it fits:** Medias24 says shopkeepers hide pads and the #7achak campaign says asking is shameful. Mothers also appear in the demand videos above.
- **Risks:** Meta and TikTok ad rules for period-related content are unverified. Keep claims to "discreet" and "pouch", no health claims. Do not include pads or tampons in the box without checking hygiene-product rules.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Check Moroccan competitors.** Search Jumia, Avito, Instagram and TikTok in Arabic ("جيب الفوطة", "بوشيط") and French ("pochette serviette hygiénique"). Look at Wlidaty and Marjane Mall. I did not find a pouch seller, but search is not proof.
2. **Ask 20 women, 15-30, in a WhatsApp poll:** "Where do you carry pads at school, work or the gym? What have you used? Would you pay X for a discreet pouch?" Offer 3 designs. Do not mention products.
3. **Pre-order test:** a plain Instagram post of the pouch as a "mini case" (no period words), 10 pre-orders with COD. Check how Meta handles the ad before you spend.
