## Solution ideas
**A. An extra-large claw clip for thick hair with a reinforced spring.**
- **What:** large size, metal-reinforced spring, textured or double teeth, matte finish.
- **Why it fits:** the main complaints are broken springs, too-small clips and weak hold.
- **Keywords:** 大号 抓夹 加强弹簧 浓密头发, 鲨鱼夹 加大 防滑 双排齿 / "extra large claw clip thick hair strong spring", "jumbo claw clip textured". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** quality control (spring failure). Test it: open and close 50 times and hold a thick ponytail for an hour. A crowded, price-driven market.

**B. A gentle hair-tie set: spiral plus wide fabric scrunchies.**
- **What:** a mixed pack in 2 sizes for thick hair.
- **Why it fits:** stylists and brand sites recommend spiral and fabric ties over plain elastics.
- **Keywords:** 螺旋 发圈 电话线 不伤发, 大号 缎面 发圈 / "spiral hair ties", "large satin scrunchie".
- **Risks:** very crowded and low price. Do not claim "prevents breakage" or any hair-health benefit.

**C. Service: a replacement promise and a hair-volume size guide.**
- **What:** "if the spring breaks in 60 days we replace it" (the exact period is up to you), plus a simple guide to clip size by hair volume.
- **Why it fits:** breaking after a few wears is the most repeated complaint. A promise is a real difference in a market of cheap packs.
- **Risks:** replacement cost and abuse. Needs a clip you trust first. No customer gets this unless the clip is good.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Read the Jumia listings by hand.** Check the 1-2 star reviews on the best-selling claw clips and note repeated words (breaks, too small, slips). Check Instagram sellers and Facebook Ad Library. I could not read review text.
2. **Test clips yourself.** Buy 3 popular Jumia clips and 3 samples from 1688. Do the 50-cycle open-close test and a one-hour thick-hair hold test. Film a 10-second hold-and-shake demo.
3. **Ask 20 women with thick or curly hair:** "How many clips have you broken this year? What do you use? What would make you pay more?" Take 10 pre-orders for the winner.
