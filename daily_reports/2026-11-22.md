## Solution ideas
**A. A thin, cool "under-skirt" short, not shapewear.**
- **What:** light cotton-modal or similar short in two lengths (mid-thigh and knee), flat seams, a soft waistband that does not roll, neutral tones.
- **Why it fits:** the complaints are heat, thickness and rolling. Jumia's offer is mostly thick, compressing shapewear. (My inference from titles, not tested.)
- **Keywords:** 防走光 安全裤 女 薄款 莫代尔, 防磨腿 打底短裤 / "anti chafing shorts women thin", "slip shorts modal under dress". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** size returns on COD, crowded category, competition on price. Say "barrier layer", not "prevents irritation".

**B. Thigh bands that stay put.**
- **What:** wide lace-trim bands with silicone grip stripes, pack of 2 or 3, in several skin tones, sold by thigh size.
- **Why it fits:** bands are popular abroad, but slipping is the main complaint.
- **Keywords:** 防磨腿带 大腿 防滑 硅胶 蕾丝, 大腿防摩擦带 / "anti chafe thigh bands lace silicone".
- **Risks:** thigh size range, skin-tone range, silicone quality. Test sweaty walking before promising "stays put".

**C. Service: measurement and match helper.**
- **What:** a WhatsApp check: send thigh and hip measurements plus your skirt length, and get the length and size. Skin-tone match by photo.
- **Why it fits:** sizing and tone mismatch drive returns on both A and B.
- **Risk:** your time. Does not fix the product.

**Timing note (my guess):** summer is about 6 months away. Sourcing from China plus shipping might take weeks, so order samples early in the year. Check this with your supplier.
