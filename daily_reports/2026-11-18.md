# Daily problem report: 2026-11-18

## Problem
**Women's clothes have no usable pockets, and the small phone bags that replace them have straps that are too short and pockets too small for a phone in a case.**

## Who has it, how often, how painful
- **Who:** women who want to go out with just a phone, cards and keys. Daily.
- **No Moroccan woman's quote found on pockets.** I found no Moroccan source about djellaba, abaya or dress pockets. Everything below is from other countries (proxy), except the Moroccan bag-theft news.
- **Measured fact (proxy, US/EU jeans):** a 2018 study of men's and women's jeans from 20 brands found women's front pockets about 46% shorter and 10% narrower. Only 5% of women's pockets could carry a Google Pixel, against 85% of men's. Only 10% fit the wearer's hand. [Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/study-confirms-womens-pockets-are-too-small-for-smartphones), [Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/news/iphone-pocket-women-jeans-too-small) (coverage of one small sample).
- **Poll (proxy, UK):** 38% of women put clothes back once they notice there are no pockets. Trousers are where women want pockets most. [YouGov](https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/32765-what-women-want-pockets-they-can-use)
- **Repeated complaints about the phone-bag fix (proxy, US reviews of 6 different phone crossbody bags, mid-to-high priced brands, read as snippets in search results):**
  - **Strap too short, 5 reviewers.** "the strap feels a tiny bit too short" (Kate Spade), "even after I extended it the full length it's still too short" (Baggallini), "short" (Macy's), "SHORT" (Michael Kors).
  - **Phone with a case does not fit, 3 reviewers.** "your phone can't have a case on it" (Kate Spade), fits "with no room to spare" (Michael Kors), "too small... I needed more depth" for phone plus keys and glasses (Baggallini).
  - **Too small overall, 2 reviewers** (Baggallini, Levenger).
  - **Strap or buckle broke, 1-2.** "strap broke quickly" (J.Crew).
  - **Things fall out when walking, 1.** (Baggallini)

## Solution ideas
**A. A phone crossbody pouch built around the review complaints.**
- **What:** long, adjustable strap; deeper pocket that fits a phone in a case plus cards and keys; secure zip; two sizes by phone size.
- **Why it fits:** targets the repeated complaints: short strap, no room for a case, too small, fragile strap.
- **Keywords:** 手机斜挎包 女 可调节肩带, 迷你斜挎包 手机袋, 手机包 加长肩带 / "phone crossbody bag women adjustable strap", "mini phone pouch zipper". Verify before relying on them.
- **Risks:** exact phone-plus-case sizing, strap and hardware quality, zipper. Small and light. Do not claim "anti-theft".

**B. Hidden pouch worn under the djellaba, abaya or dress.** *(Hypothesis: no Moroccan evidence found.)*
- **What:** a slim soft pouch on a waist band or strap, worn inside the clothes.
- **Why it might fit:** flowing garments may have no pockets, and the heat makes a bag extra work. I did not find women saying this.
- **Keywords:** 隐形腰包 手机 贴身, 内穿腰包 女 / "hidden waist pouch under clothes", "inner belt pouch phone".
- **Risks:** comfort in heat, sweat, bulge under tight clothes, hard to demo on video. Check by asking women first.

**C. Service: "send your phone model, we tell you the size."**
- **What:** a WhatsApp fit check before COD, plus a size guide by phone model and case.
- **Why it fits:** the 3 "doesn't fit with a case" complaints. It cuts COD returns, a real cost.
- **Risk:** your time. It does not fix a weak strap.

## How to find out if it works (3 cheap steps)
1. **Check Moroccan competitors.** Open Jumia's waist-bag and phone-pouch pages, Instagram sellers ("sac téléphone", "حقيبة هاتف") and Facebook Ad Library. Write down prices in MAD and the 1-2 star reviews you can see. I could not open Jumia pages beyond search snippets.
2. **Test samples on camera.** Buy 2-3 best-selling pouches and 2 samples from 1688. Test with a phone in a case plus cards and keys. Walk 10 minutes and film it: strap length, fit, zip. Keep only the winner.
3. **Ask 20 women (18-30) by WhatsApp:** "When your outfit has no pockets, where does your phone go?" Include djellaba, abaya, dress, jeans. Then offer 10 pre-orders for the winner.
