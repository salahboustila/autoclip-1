# Problems with no good solution: women 18-30, Morocco

Date: 2026-11-15. Research only. No product picked.
Older version of this report: `women_problems_research_v1.md`.

## Read this first

**What this report is:** problems backed by a real quote or a real workaround, each with a link. Where I found nothing, I say "no evidence found".

**How strong is the evidence? Honest summary**
- **Weak overall.** Only 3 problems have a repeated complaint (3+ voices). Most rows rest on one video, one news article or a survey.
- **Very little is from Moroccan women.** Most real quotes are TikTok comments. Many are from Algeria, the Gulf or elsewhere. I label every source: **[MA]** Moroccan, **[Maghreb]** Algerian or similar dialect, **[proxy]** non-Moroccan.
- **My own search choices bias the result.** I searched hijab topics on purpose in earlier rounds, so hijab problems look bigger than they may be. Treat that as a lead, not a verdict.
- **Translations are mine.** Arabic and Darija quotes are short and approximate.

**What I could not reach** (the main reason the evidence is thin)
- Jumia Morocco and Avito answer 403 to automated requests. I did not work around that. The few Jumia listings seen through search have almost no reviews (see Section 4).
- Amazon shows a bot check. Reddit answers 403. Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities are private or need a login. Instagram comments cannot be read.
- No Moroccan women's forum turned up in search.
- What worked: web search, TikTok comments and TikTok/Instagram video data through vidIQ, and some news sites.

**Evidence grade (my own, goes in the Problem column)**
- **A** = same complaint from 3+ different voices, or a real workaround plus repeated complaint.
- **B** = one real workaround or voice, or a survey or news report.
- **C** = brand or retailer blog only.

## Section 2. Top 5 shortlist (ranked by evidence strength, not by scores)

1. **Hijab or long top clings to trousers (#1).** The strongest repeated complaint I found: 5+ voices say the common fix failed, with real workarounds named. Weakness: Algerian dialect, one video, and it is unclear what product would solve it.
2. **Hijab fixing accessory is overpriced and worrying (#2).** 5+ people say the price is too high, one Moroccan gives a number (415 MAD), and others name specific fears (cutting fabric, hair tangling). Local cheap alternatives are priced around 39 MAD for 12 pins (Jumia, per search).
3. **Leggings waistband rolls down (#6).** The only repeated complaint in actual product reviews (4 reviewers, same category). Weakness: US reviews, not Moroccan.
4. **Heels slip and hurt (#4).** A real Moroccan workaround (napkin in the boot) with 2.6M views, plus a known cause. Weakness: no complaint quote.
5. **Hijab pins hurt, get lost, feel unsafe (#3).** Many buy-intent comments (6+) and millions of views, with a Moroccan story of a pin accident. Weakness: overlaps with #2.

**Watch out:** three of the five are hijab-related. That may be one customer segment, not three problems. It may also come from my earlier searches. Consider checking non-hijab problems with the WhatsApp poll in Section 5.

## Section 5. Five questions to answer before the brainstorm

1. **How many of your target customers wear hijab?** If most do, three of the top five matter. If few, move to #6 and #4.
2. **What is your landed cost target and price ceiling in MAD?** The price complaints (415 MAD "too expensive", pins at 39 MAD for 12) suggest a clear ceiling.
3. **Can you get 20-30 women (18-30) to answer a 5-question WhatsApp poll this week?** It is the fastest way to fill the Moroccan evidence gap.
4. **Are period items and safety items off the table?** Both carry claim and legal risk. Say yes or no so I drop them.
5. **Who is the customer exactly?** Students or workers, big city (tram, bus) or smaller towns, and which channel (TikTok, Instagram, COD). Heat, transport and budget differ a lot.
