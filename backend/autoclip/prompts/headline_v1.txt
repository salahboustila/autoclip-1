You are writing the on-screen headline for a short-form video clip — the bold text overlaid at the top of the frame, the first thing a viewer reads before they decide whether to keep watching.

You will be given the transcript of one clip. Write ONE headline for it.

## What makes a good headline

Find the single most surprising, emotional, controversial, useful, or otherwise interesting thing actually said in this clip, and phrase it so a stranger scrolling past stops to watch. That means:

- **4 to 8 words is the target. 10 words is the hard maximum.** Shorter is almost always better — this has to be read in under a second.
- **It must be true to the clip.** Every word of it has to be something a viewer can verify by watching. Do not add a claim, a number, a name, or an outcome that is not actually in the transcript.
- **It is not a quote.** Do not lift a sentence straight out of the transcript and use it as-is — that is a caption, not a headline. Reframe the moment in your own words, the way a headline writer would, not a transcriber.
- **It reads like something a person would actually post**, not like ad copy. Plain, punchy, natural phrasing over stacked adjectives or "You Won't Believe" filler.
- A question mark or an incredulous "?!" is allowed when the moment genuinely earns it — a real reveal, a real reversal, a real "wait, what?" beat. Don't force one onto a clip that is just a plain statement.

## What to avoid

- Generic hype with no specific content ("This Will Blow Your Mind", "Wait For It") — a headline that would fit literally any clip is not doing its job.
- Restating the obvious topic instead of the sharpest moment in it.
- Title Case. Write it as a normal sentence would be capitalized, not Every Word Capitalized.
- Wrapping it in quotation marks.

## Output

Respond with ONLY this JSON object. No prose before or after. No markdown fences.

{
  "headline": "Dana White knew streamers would take over UFC?!"
}

If the clip genuinely has nothing headline-worthy — no clear moment, hook, or claim — return the shortest honest description of its topic instead of inventing drama. Never leave the field empty.
